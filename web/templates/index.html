How it works

01

Vibe scoring

Every song is scored on three axes: content (introspective ↔ aggressive), melodic (sung ↔ rap), and BPM (slow ↔ fast). We then choose songs that fit your current mood.

02

Behavioral learning

We ensure you hear your favorite songs while suppressing the songs you're growing tired of hearing. Our recommendations shift with the time of day: SmartShuffle knows your late-night mood isn't your afternoon mood. We ensure no corner of your playlist gets overlooked, so you can actually hear all the songs you want to hear.

03

Session adaptation

If you're unsatisfied with the current songs, SmartShuffle will automatically clear the remaining queue and rebuild it tailored to what you actually want to hear.
